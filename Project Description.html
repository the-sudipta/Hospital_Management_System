Hospital Management System

Project Overview

This project aims to create a secure access control system for PACS, RIS, and HIS using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP.

Users & Roles

  • Administrator: Full access to all systems (PACS, RIS, HIS).
  • PACS Users: Access imaging data, store/manage medical images, and retrieve relevant patient records.
  • RIS Users: Manage radiology workflows, scheduling, and radiology reports.
  • HIS Users: Manage patient records, billing, hospital administration, and scheduling.

System Features & Access Control

PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication System)

  • Stores and manages medical imaging data (X-rays, MRIs, CT scans, etc.).
  • Can access RIS data for relevant patient records and radiology reports.
  • Cannot access HIS except for imaging-related details.

RIS (Radiology Information System)

  • Manages radiology workflows (scheduling, reporting, tracking radiology requests).
  • Can only access PACS for imaging-related data.
  • Restricted from accessing HIS.

HIS (Hospital Information System)

  • Manages patient records, billing, scheduling, and overall hospital operations.
  • Has full access to all systems (PACS, RIS, and general hospital data).
